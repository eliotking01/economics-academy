Sample cards

State the four main objectives of government macroeconomic policy.

  • Economic growth.
  • Price stability (low and stable inflation — the UK's 2% CPI target).
  • Minimising unemployment.
  • A stable balance of payments on current account.

Be aware of further objectives too — balancing the government budget, a more equitable distribution of income, and environmental protection — and that the importance attached to each objective changes over time.

State the formulas for the unemployment rate and the employment rate.

Unemploymentrate=Unemployed/Labourforce×100Unemployment rate = Unemployed / Labour force × 100Unemploymentrate=Unemployed/Labourforce×100

The unemployment rate is the percentage of the labour force unemployed and actively seeking work. The employment rate is the percentage of the working-age population in work.

The denominators differ — someone economically inactive is in neither numerator, so the two rates can move independently.

Draw the circular flow of income for a simple two-agent economy.

Households and firms are linked by two pairs of flows.

Real flows: households provide the factors of production — land, labour, capital, enterprise — and firms provide goods and services.

Money flows: firms pay income — rent, wages, interest, profit — and households return it as consumer expenditure, which becomes firms' revenue.

The same flow measured at any point gives income = output = expenditure.
