Quick Answers

Are the revision notes really free?

Yes — the notes, flashcards, practice questions, past papers, question finder and glossary are all completely free, with no account or sign-up needed. Tutoring and marking are the only paid services on the site.

Which exam boards are covered?

Revision notes, flashcards and practice questions cover Edexcel A and AQA. Past papers cover Edexcel A, AQA, Edexcel B and OCR, each with mark schemes.

What is the difference between the practice questions and the question finder?

The practice questions are original questions written for this site, so you can test yourself topic by topic without using up real papers. The question finder searches genuine past paper questions and links you to the exact paper and mark scheme.

How does tutoring work?

Lessons are online: 1-to-1 at £65 an hour, or small groups of 2–4 students at £35 an hour per student. It starts with a free 15-minute intro call so you can see if it is a good fit before paying anything.
