1.1.2 The Nature and Purpose of Economic Activity

The Central Purpose of Economic Activity

Specification Coverage: AQA unit 1.1.2 - The Nature and Purpose of Economic Activity. Students must learn about the central purpose of economic activity, and the fundamental decisions that every economy must make in order to allocate scarce resources efficiently.

The central purpose of economic activity is to produce goods and services to satisfy the needs and wants of consumers.

  • Needs: Basic requirements for human survival, such as food, water, and shelter.
  • Wants: Desires for goods and services that improve quality of life, such as entertainment, luxury items, and travel.

Fundamental Economic Decisions

Every economy must make fundamental decisions about:

  • What to produce: Deciding which goods and services to produce based on consumer demand and resource availability.
  • How to produce: Determining the methods of production, including the choice of technology and allocation of resources.
  • For whom to produce: Deciding how goods and services will be distributed among the population, which can be influenced by income distribution, social policies, and market mechanisms.
