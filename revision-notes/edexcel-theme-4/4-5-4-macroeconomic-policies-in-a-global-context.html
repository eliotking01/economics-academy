Macroeconomic Policies in a Global Context

These Edexcel A-Level Economics revision notes cover unit 4.5.4, examining how globalisation constrains national macroeconomic policy — through capital mobility, exchange rate pressures, and international policy coordination — and evaluating the options available to policymakers in an open economy.

Specification Coverage: Edexcel unit 4.5.4 - Macroeconomic Policies in a Global Context. Students should be able to explain how globalisation constrains national macroeconomic policy, analyse policy responses to major global challenges, evaluate the challenges of controlling global firms, and assess the trade-offs facing policymakers in an interconnected world.

Exam Preparation

  • Keep the core theme in mind: in a global economy, no country is an island.
  • Evaluate policy effectiveness by asking how global factors might limit or alter the outcome of a national policy.
  • Use case studies such as post-2008 austerity, post-COVID inflation, and global tax deals.
  • Conclude with trade-offs by explaining that successful policy often depends on international cooperation and careful judgement between competing goals.
