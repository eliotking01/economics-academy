My Story

I am a First-Class BSc Economics graduate from the University of Bath, having previously achieved four A* grades in my A-Levels. Since completing my studies, I have gained over 24 months' experience in London, working at a hedge fund and a FinTech startup.

Alongside my university studies and finance career, I never stopped teaching Economics, and during this time I have supported over 100 students towards achieving their target grades. More recently, it has been my pleasure to teach Economics full time, while founding my own business, Economics Academy, to further support my students in their studies.

None of this would have been possible without the tuition I received in preparation for my 11+ entrance exam. My tutor supported me where my school could not, enabling me to access a high-quality Grammar School Education which proved invaluable in achieving my goals.
