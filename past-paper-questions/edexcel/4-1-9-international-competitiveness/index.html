In 2022 the value of UK exports fell by 14% compared with 2020. In contrast, the global average increase in exports was 8.2% over the same time period. The UK’s competitiveness also fell, dropping five places from 18th in 2021 to 23rd in 2022 in the world competitiveness rankings. (Sources: adapted from https://www.ft.com/content/021c629d-5853-4111-9600-ab5f0eb65a35 and https://www.imd.org/centers/world-competitiveness-center/rankings/world-competitiveness) Evaluate macroeconomic policies that could be used to increase international competitiveness in the UK’s export markets.
