Common Questions

What happens after I pay?

You'll land on a confirmation page with one simple job: email your work to Eliot. Your turnaround starts as soon as both your payment and your work have arrived — and your feedback comes back to the email address you paid with. Bought a bundle? Your three papers don't have to arrive together — each one's turnaround runs from when you send it.

How do I send handwritten work?

PDF scans are preferred — a free phone scanning app works perfectly — but clear photos are absolutely fine too. Typed work can come as a Word document or PDF. Whatever you send, just make sure every page is readable.

Which exam boards do you cover?

All of them: Edexcel A, Edexcel B, AQA and OCR — every paper and every question type. Just tell Eliot which board and paper your work is from when you send it.

What exactly do I get back?

Three things: a mark and a grade, an annotated PDF of your paper showing where you earned and lost marks across Knowledge, Application, Analysis and Evaluation, and a detailed follow-up email with rewritten versions of your points showing how to score higher.

Who does the marking?

All marking is done personally by Eliot King — First-Class BSc Economics from the University of Bath, with 6 years of tutoring experience and 100+ students taught. Find out more on the About page.

What if my question type isn't listed?

Email your enquiry and Eliot will reply with a personal quote — data response questions, mixed submissions and regular ongoing marking are all available. See the enquiry box above the examples section.
