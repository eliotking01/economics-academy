Self-hosted web fonts - economicsacademy.co.uk
==============================================

Added 2026-08-23 (performance pass, Phase 2). Until then every page linked a
Google Fonts stylesheet; these are the same faces, served from this origin.

Every .woff2 here is the static latin-subset file Google Fonts itself serves
(fetched 2026-08-23 from fonts.gstatic.com, as delivered to a browser without
variable-font support), unmodified:

  source-sans-pro-300 / -300-italic / -400 / -700 / -900   Source Sans Pro v23
  open-sans-400 / -600 / -700                             Open Sans v44
  merriweather-400 / -400-italic / -700                    Merriweather v33

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 - see
OFL-sourcesanspro.txt (Adobe), OFL-opensans.txt, OFL-merriweather.txt.

fa-solid-900.woff2 is a subset of Font Awesome Free 5.15.4 (its fonts are
SIL OFL 1.1); scripts/subset_fontawesome.py regenerates it.

The @font-face rules are in css/main.css (Source Sans Pro, Open Sans) and in
css/pages/revision-notes-textbook.css and css/pages/quiz.css (Merriweather).
Do not swap Source Sans Pro for Source Sans 3 - the glyphs differ.
