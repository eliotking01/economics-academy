Common Questions

How much does an A-Level Economics tutor cost?

One-to-one lessons are £65 per hour and small group lessons are £35 per hour per student. Specialist A-Level Economics tuition in the UK typically costs £40 to £90+ per hour, so both options are competitively priced — and the 15-minute introductory call is always free.

Which exam boards do you cover?

Edexcel A, AQA, Edexcel B and OCR. Lessons are tailored to your exact specification — its content, command words and mark schemes — and backed by the free board-specific revision notes and past papers on this site.

How do online lessons work?

Lessons take place on thelessonspace.com, an interactive online classroom with video, a shared whiteboard and live document editing. There is nothing to install and no account to create — once you book, you receive an email and calendar invite with your lesson link.

How do group lessons work?

Groups have two to four students on the same exam board and level. Join with friends as a ready-made group, or sign up individually and be matched into a suitable group. You pay for just your first group lesson to try it; after that, places are billed per half-term or term so the group stays stable.

What happens in a typical session?

Sessions are personalised to each student's needs, focusing on building understanding, improving essay technique, and practising exam-style questions with detailed feedback.

Can I change or cancel sessions?

Yes! You can reschedule up to 24 hours before your session. Lessons cancelled less than 24h in advance will be subject to a 100% lesson cancellation fee.

Are you DBS checked?

Yes — I hold an Enhanced DBS certificate. You can read more about my qualifications and experience on the About page.

Do you offer more than tutoring?

Yes. There is a paper-marking service with examiner-style feedback on essays and full papers, plus free revision notes, past papers, flashcards and practice questions for every topic.
