## Three things this surfaced that you may want to fix in the notes

1. **Edexcel has no general definition of "Regulation".** All three Edexcel
   sources describe applying regulation to an externality. The glossary
   currently shows Edexcel readers `1.4.1` — *"bans/limits on the production of
   demerit goods…"* — which is an example, not a definition. AQA has a proper
   one at `1.8.8`. A chip on an Edexcel page would fix it.

2. **AQA has no general definition of "Subsidies".** Same shape in reverse:
   AQA's only source is `1.8.4`, the externality application. Edexcel has the
   real definition at `1.2.9`.

3. **Edexcel's merit and demerit good definitions are the weaker pair.**
   Edexcel only has the `1.3.2` wording, which attributes the external cost to
   "the producer". For the standard demerit goods that is the wrong party.
   AQA's `1.8.5` wording is better on both counts and Edexcel readers do not
   get it. Worth aligning `1.3.2` with `1.8.5`.

None of these has been changed. They need a notes edit and your instruction.
